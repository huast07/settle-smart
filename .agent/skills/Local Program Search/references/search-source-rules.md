# Newcomer Local Program Search Rules

Load this reference when editing search prompts, search skills, query generation, eligibility extraction, verification, or signup extraction.

## Search Categories

Search across prioritized essentials:

- grocery and food
- transit and mobility
- banking and no-fee accounts
- credit cards and credit building for adults
- commercial pharmacy loyalty and prescription savings
- government healthcare, coverage, or prescription benefit programs
- housing, rent rewards, utility discounts, and tenant benefits
- cellular, prepaid mobile, internet, affordability programs, multi-line offers, and bundles
- household, family, senior, child, student/youth, and bulk purchasing programs when profile-supported

## Source Priority

Prefer:

1. official provider signup or program pages
2. government health agency, benefits portal, city/province/state, or transit agency pages
3. official retailer, grocery, pharmacy, bank, credit issuer, utility, telecom, internet provider pages
4. official help-center pages that confirm eligibility or signup
5. third-party aggregators only as discovery leads that point back to official sources

Do not rely on forums, coupon blogs, or stale aggregator pages as final evidence.

## Query Patterns

Build destination-specific queries, for example:

- `<destination> grocery loyalty program signup official`
- `<destination> transit fare discount pass official`
- `<destination> newcomer banking account bonus official`
- `<destination> secured credit card newcomer no annual fee official`
- `<destination> commercial pharmacy rewards prescription savings official`
- `<destination> government healthcare benefits newcomer official`
- `<destination> mobile phone plan rewards discount official`
- `<destination> internet affordability program telecom official`

For grocery results, first identify named chains serving the destination, then search each chain's official loyalty, member pricing, rewards, flyer, or coupon page.

## Evidence To Capture

For each candidate:

- program name
- provider
- category
- source URL when available
- short evidence summary
- eligibility notes
- signup path
- cost, fees, credit check, contract, device, or documentation caveats
- service-area or location limits
- confidence

## Safety Boundaries

- Do not invent programs, URLs, rewards, fees, or signup steps.
- Do not infer immigration status, income, medical eligibility, family relationships, or credit eligibility unless source evidence says so.
- Distinguish commercial loyalty/rewards programs from public benefits.
- For healthcare, describe official application or benefit information without medical advice.
- For banking and credit, flag fees and checks without financial advice.
